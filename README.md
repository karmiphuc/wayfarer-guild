# Wayfarer Guild

Personal autonomous management RPG / roguelite prototype inspired by the management loops of Dungeon Village and Kingdom Adventurers, with original code, names, text and visuals.

Live: https://karmiphuc.github.io/wayfarer-guild/

## v0.3

- Autonomous residents and visitors with needs, satisfaction, work and housing
- Utility-driven eating, rest, training, shopping, farming, exploration and combat
- Ranged, melee, magic, healing and tank-style combat behavior
- KO and rescue-to-inn behavior
- Field monsters that roam autonomously
- Roaming boss invasions that move toward town and pressure popularity
- Jobs, job levels, mastery, permanent stat growth and inherited mastery skills
- Equipment rarity plus dungeon blueprint discovery feeding town shop stock
- Autonomous resident gear shopping and auto-equip
- One active quest at a time
- Physical dungeon map with autonomous party pathing, fights, healing, camps, treasure, elites and bosses
- Village popularity, Town Points, rank progression and evaluations
- Buildable/upgradable facilities with quality, appeal, income and upkeep
- Research-gated buildings and careers
- Frontier fog-of-war deployment and resource gathering
- Shining monsters, eggs, Monster Stable incubation and assignable companions
- Rideable companions that become mounts after bonding
- iPad/mobile responsive layout
- Local autosave plus JSON export/import

## Design constraint

The project targets close mechanical and pacing parity with its inspirations while keeping expressive assets, writing, names and UI artwork original.
